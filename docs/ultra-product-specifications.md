## RESIDENTIAL / COMMERCIAL FENCE STYLES

### UAF-200 — Flat Top (3-Rail)
- **Style:** Clean flat top rail, 3-rail design
- **Grades:** Residential, Residential Premium, Commercial, Commercial Premium
- **Heights:** 3', 3-1/2', 4', 4-1/2', 5', 6'
- **Section Width:** 6' (custom available)
- **Pickets:**
  - Residential: 5/8" square x .050 wall
  - Commercial: 3/4" square x .055 wall
- **Picket Spacing:**
  - Residential: 3-13/16" standard | 1-5/8" optional
  - Commercial: 3-5/8" standard | 1-1/2" optional
- **Rails:**
  - Residential: 1-1/8" x 1" (.080 side walls, .062 top walls)
  - Commercial: 1-1/4" x 1-3/8" (.088 side walls, .065 top walls)
- **Posts:**
  - Residential: 2" sq (.060, .080, .125) or 2-1/2" sq (.100)
  - Commercial: 2" sq (.080, .125), 2-1/2" sq (.100), 3" sq (.125)
- **Pool Code:** Available as UAF-200 Flush (2-rail) for pool compliance
- **Rackable:** Yes (Standard 0-6", Rackable 0-20", Heavy Rack 0-36")
- **Gate Options:** Walk (UAF-200 W), Drive (UAF-200 D), Estate (UAF-200 D Estate)
- **Top Rail Strength:** Residential 350 lbs, Commercial 500 lbs, Industrial 1,000 lbs

## GATE TYPES & SPECIFICATIONS

### Walk Gates (Single Leaf)
- **How it works:** Single leaf hinged to one post, latched to opposite post
- **Available Models:**
  - UAF-200 W (Standard & Arched)
  - UAF-250 W with Spears (Standard & Arched)
  - UAB-200 W Flush (Standard & Arched)
  - UAS-100 W Spear Top (Standard & Arched)
  - UAS-150 W Staggered Spear (Standard & Arched)
- **Sizes:**
  - Residential: Widths 36", 42", 48" | Heights 3', 3-1/2', 4', 4-1/2', 5', 6'
  - Commercial: Widths 36", 42", 48" | Heights 4', 5', 6'
  - Industrial: Widths up to 72" | Heights 4'-8'
- **Grades:** Residential, Res Premium, Commercial, Comm Premium, Industrial, Ind Premium

### Drive Gates (Double Leaf)
- **How it works:** Two leaves latching together, hinged separately on opposite sides
- **Available Models:**
  - UAF-200 D (Standard & Arched)
  - UAF-250 D with Spears (Standard & Arched)
  - UAS-100 D Spear Top (Standard & Arched)
  - UAS-150 D Staggered Spear (Standard & Arched)
- **Max Widths:**
  - Residential: Up to 12' wide
  - Commercial: Up to 16' wide
  - Industrial: Up to 28' wide
- **Heights:** 4'-8'
- **Grades:** All six grades

### Estate Gates (Industrial Drive Gate)
- **How it works:** Compound bowed upper horizontal rails for decorative rolling appearance
- **Available Models:**
  - Estate Gate UAF-200 D
  - Estate Gate UAF-250 D
  - Estate Gate UAS-100
  - Estate Gate UAS-150
- **Sizes:** 10'-28' wide | 4'-8' high
- **Compatible with:** Ultra Access Controls gate openers

### Cantilever Gates (Rolling/Sliding)
- **How it works:** Gate rolls on wheels within a track, designed for one-hand operation
- **Sizes:** 6'-50' wide | 4', 5', 6', 7', 8' high (custom available)
- **Grade:** Industrial only
- **Frame:** 2" square aluminum tubing x 1/8" thickness (vertical and diagonal)
- **Posts:** 4" schedule 40 galvanized x .125 thickness
- **Track System:**
  - Two heavy-duty swivel trucks
  - Four horizontal wheels with factory-sealed/lubricated bearings
  - Two high-impact lateral wheels
  - Top tracks conceal truck assembly (weather protection)
  - Single and double track models available
- **Features:**
  - Y-shaped gate receiver
  - Compatible with automatic gate openers and security systems
  - One-hand operation even on largest gates
- **Compliance:** ASTM F-1184, F-626, A-153
- **Warranty:** 5-year limited (cantilever specific)

### Cantilevered Estate Gate (Hybrid)
- Combines cantilever mechanics with estate gate aesthetics

### All Gate Construction:
- 100% welded at all connections and joints
- Stainless steel fasteners
- High-strength Ultrum 6005-T5 alloy
- Powercoat finish
- Customizable with Designer Accessories (scrolls, butterflies, circles, finials)
- Available in 7 standard colors + custom

## RACKABILITY

Ultra Aluminum offers three rackability tiers at **no extra charge**:

| Tier | Grade Change Per 6' Panel | Method |
|------|--------------------------|--------|
| Standard | 0-6" | Standard punched holes |
| Rackable | 0-20" | Double-punched horizontal rails |
| Heavy Rack | 0-36" | Triple-punched horizontal rails |

- Posts are punched with larger holes (not around pickets) for racking
- Rackability allows fence to follow inclines and declines
- Maintains level and consistent top/bottom line on uneven terrain

## MATERIALS & FINISH

### Alloy
- **Type:** Ultrum 6005-T5 aluminum alloy
- **Strength:** Minimum 35,000 PSI (comparable to steel)
- **Properties:** Will never rust

### Rail Construction
- 20% more aluminum than competitors
- Full 8 ribs inside each rail
- Premium grades: Reinforced double-wall rail with hidden fasteners (2-piece fully encased rail)

### Finish: Powercoat
- Powder coat meeting **AAMA 2604** specifications
- Twice the thickness and hardness of typical acrylic, baked enamel, or wet paint
- More durable, fade-resistant, and scratch-resistant
- **Lifetime warranty** on finish

### Fasteners
- All stainless steel

### Assembly
- Sections arrive fully factory pre-assembled in 6' lengths
- Gates are 100% welded at all connections and joints

### Warranty
- **Lifetime Limited Warranty** on fence and railing
- **5-Year Limited Warranty** on cantilever gates
- Covers defects in workmanship and materials
