## 1. Federal baseline — IRC / ISPSC

The **International Residential Code (IRC)** governs one- and two-family dwellings. The **International Swimming Pool and Spa Code (ISPSC)**, first published 2012 and updated on the 3-year ICC cycle (2012, 2015, 2018, 2021, 2024), is the authoritative pool barrier code referenced by most states.

### 1a. Pool barrier — IRC Appendix G / ISPSC §305

| Requirement | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum barrier height** | 48 in. above grade (outside side) | IRC Appendix G §AG105.2 / ISPSC §305.2.1 |
| **Height maintained** | Within a 3 ft horizontal arc from the barrier face (for grade slope) | ISPSC §305.2.1 |
| **Max gap under barrier — solid surface** | 4 in. | ISPSC §305.2.2 |
| **Max gap under barrier — grass/gravel** | 2 in. | ISPSC §305.2.2 |
| **Openings in barrier (sphere test)** | No passage of 4 in. sphere | ISPSC §305.3 |
| **Vertical member spacing (if horiz < 45 in. apart)** | ≤ 1¾ in. between verticals | ISPSC §305.3.1 |
| **Decorative cutouts** | ≤ 1¾ in. wide | ISPSC §305.3.2 |
| **Diagonal members (chain-link/lattice)** | ≤ 1¾ in. openings | ISPSC §305.3.4 |
| **Horizontal climbability rule** | If two horizontals are < 45 in. apart, they must be on the pool side | ISPSC §305.3.3 |
| **Gate — self-closing** | Required | ISPSC §305.4.3 |
| **Gate — self-latching** | Required | ISPSC §305.4.3 |
| **Gate — swing direction** | Outward, away from pool | ISPSC §305.4.1 |
| **Gate latch height (release outside)** | ≥ 54 in. above ground | ISPSC §305.4.3.1 |
| **Gate latch height (release pool side, alt)** | ≥ 3 in. below top of gate, with no openings > ½ in. within 18 in. radius of release | ISPSC §305.4.3.1 |
| **Door/window from dwelling into pool area** | Alarm or ASTM F1346-rated safety cover | ISPSC §305.7 |

**Sources:** Fine Homebuilding 2022-09-02 article (summary of ISPSC §305); ICC codes.iccsafe.org IRC Appendix G index.

### 1b. General fence — IRC §R301 (structural/wind load) + local zoning

Ornamental aluminum fences are almost never governed by IRC directly unless they (a) form part of a pool barrier (see §1a) or (b) act as a guard on a deck/balcony (IRC §R312 — requires 36 in. min on decks ≥ 30 in. above grade, 4 in. sphere rule under R312.1.3). **All other fence rules — height limits, setbacks, HOA approval — are in local zoning ordinances, not the IRC.**

**Typical municipal fence zoning (not IRC):**
- Front yard: 3–4 ft max height
- Side/rear yard: 6 ft max height without permit, 8 ft with permit
- Corner-lot sight triangle: 30-in. max height within specified dimensions from intersection
- Setback from property line: 0–12 in. typical (varies wildly)

Our public-facing card for "General Fence" should state federal code applies only to pool and deck cases; everything else is local.

### 1c. Railing / guard — IRC §R312

Applies when a deck or landing is more than 30 in. above grade or floor below. Not typically relevant for ground-level yard fencing but worth including as an education card for customers building decks + fence combos.

| Requirement | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum height | 36 in. (residential) | IRC R312.1.2 |
| Opening sphere test | No passage of 4 in. sphere | IRC R312.1.3 |
| Sphere test (stairs, 6 in. exception) | 6 in. max at triangular opening formed by riser/tread/rail | IRC R312.1.3 Exception 1 |
| Load rating | 200 lb concentrated load, any direction on top rail | IRC R301.5 |

## 3. Michigan — pool barrier amendments

Michigan adopts the **Michigan Residential Code 2015** (adapted from IRC 2015) with state-specific amendments. Pool barriers are also enforced via **Mich. Admin. Code R 325.2128** and local city ordinances.

### 3a. Where MI aligns with federal ISPSC §305

All the federal baseline values (48 in. height, 2/4 in. ground gap, 4 in. sphere rule, 1¾ in. picket spacing under 45 in. horizontals, self-closing/latching gates swinging outward) apply.

### 3b. Where MI adds / diverges

| MI addition | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Door/window alarm** | Every door/operable window that opens directly into the fenced pool area must have an immediate-sound alarm **or** the pool is covered by an ASTM F1346-rated safety cover | Mich. Admin. Code R 325.2128; poolguardusa.com MI guide |
| **Permit requirement** | Residential building permit required for any in-ground pool; additional electrical, plumbing, fence permits may apply | Michigan Residential Code Ch. 1 |
| **Latch height strict read** | MI enforcement interpretation treats "outside-release latch ≥ 54 in." as a hard floor — not a guideline | Eaton County swimming pool booklet 2018 |

## 4. Follow-up research (later sessions)

### 4a. California — high priority, different framework

CA doesn't use IRC directly — it adopts the **California Building Code (CBC)** and **California Residential Code (CRC)** with state-specific Title 24 amendments. Pool-barrier rules are in CBC Appendix G (with CA mods) and California Health and Safety Code §§ 115920–115929.

Key things to research when we add CA:
- Two-barrier rule (Senate Bill 442 requires ≥2 of 7 enumerated safety features for new/remodeled pools)
- Senate Bill 442 safety feature list
- CBC Chapter 31B pool-specific amendments

### 4b. Texas — jurisdictional variation

Texas has no statewide pool-barrier code. Cities and counties set their own, typically based on IRC:
- Austin: adopts 2015 IRC + local amendments
- Houston: adopts 2015 IRC
- Dallas / DFW region: varies by suburb
- San Antonio: adopts 2018 IRC

When we add TX, do it at the city level, not state level.

### 4c. Florida — hurricane + pool codes

FL uses the **Florida Building Code (FBC)**, which is largely but not identically IRC-based. Two big FL-specific additions:
- Pool barrier is governed by Ch. 424 of the 2023 FBC Residential + FS § 515 (Residential Swimming Pool Safety Act)
- Fences near the coast (High-Velocity Hurricane Zone = Miami-Dade, Broward) must meet ASTM E-1886 wind-borne debris impact testing. F-2408 alone isn't enough.

## 5. Card-by-card content outline for the page

Once we start building, each card gets: category icon, title, 4 spec chips, optional ASTM badges, and "View Full Code Requirements →" deep link.

### Card: Ornamental Aluminum Fence (our primary product)
- Chips: "6 ft residential (no permit)" | "Post depth: 1/3 total length" | "Setback: verify local" | "Wind load: ASTM F-2408"
- ASTM badges: F-2408, F-626
- Body: "Residential ornamental aluminum fences up to 6 ft are generally permit-exempt under IRC §R301.1, but local zoning sets max height, setback, and corner-lot sight triangles. Check with your AHJ."

### Card: Pool Barrier (Aluminum / Vinyl / Chain-Link)
- Chips: "48″ min height" | "< 2″ bottom gap" | "4″ sphere rule" | "Self-closing gate"
- ASTM badges: F-2408, F-626
- Body: "Every pool barrier in the US follows ISPSC §305 as a baseline. 48″ minimum height, 4″ sphere-passage rule, gaps under barrier must be ≤ 2″ on grass and ≤ 4″ on concrete. Gates must be self-closing, self-latching, and swing outward. State amendments (Michigan's door/window alarm rule) may apply."

### Card: Privacy Fence (Vinyl / Aluminum)
- Chips: "6 ft max (no permit, typical)" | "Wind load: ASTM F-964" | "Post depth: 1/3 total" | "Setback: verify"
- ASTM badges: F-964 (vinyl), F-2408 (aluminum)
- Body: "Privacy fences up to 6 ft are typically permit-exempt under IRC §R301.1. Vinyl panels conform to ASTM F-964; aluminum privacy conforms to F-2408. Tall privacy (8 ft+) usually requires a permit and engineering for wind load."

### Card: Chain Link Fence (credibility card — we don't sell it)

- Body: "We don't sell chain link, but many customers compare to it. Chain-link residential installs follow ASTM F-567 with 9–11.5 gauge fabric."

### Card: Deck & Porch Railing (IRC §R312, R317, R507)
- Chips: "Min height: 36″ residential" | "Opening: 4″ sphere max" | "Load: 200 lb any direction" | "Stair exception: 6″ sphere"
- Body: "When your deck or landing sits more than 30 in. above grade, IRC R312 kicks in: 36 in. minimum rail height, 4 in. sphere rule with a 6 in. exception at stair openings, 200 lb concentrated load test. R317 covers wood-to-ground contact protection; R507 covers deck-ledger connections."

### Card: Decorative Iron (fits alongside Ornamental Aluminum)
- Chips: "ASTM F-2408 conformance" | "6 ft max residential (typical)" | "Wind load per jurisdiction" | "Not for pool barriers unless specified"
